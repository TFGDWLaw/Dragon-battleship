Battleship Command Main Menu

Place dragon warships with the mouse. Press R to rotate.

HOW TO PLAY

  1. Place your dragon warships on Friendly Waters.
  2. Use R to rotate the current ship.
  3. Click a cell in Enemy Waters to fire.
  4. A hit lets you fire again. A miss changes the turn.
  5. Sink all enemy ships to win.
